## Summary

- Usage-based billing will charge per label bought, not per API call or by volume tier.
- Rate quotes stay free, so customers who show rates at checkout aren't charged for browsing.
- Maya leads the project. Owen wants a free tier discussed separately.

## Transcript

**Maya Chen:** Three options. Per label bought, per API call, or flat volume tiers like today.

**Sam Reyes:** Customers think in labels. Nobody knows how many API calls they make.

**Owen Brandt:** Per call makes revenue hard to predict. A checkout page can quote rates a hundred times for one order.

**Hana Novak:** And tiers are what customers complain about now. They pay for a tier they only fill in December.

**Maya Chen:** So per label bought, and rate quotes stay free. Agreed?

**Owen Brandt:** Agreed. I'd like to talk about a free tier separately.

**Sam Reyes:** Agreed.
