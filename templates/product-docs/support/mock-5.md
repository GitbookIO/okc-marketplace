## Question

We cancelled a shipment after buying its label because the customer changed the order. Do we get the label fee back, and when?

## Answer

Yes. When you cancel, we ask the carrier to void the label, and the fee goes back to your balance once they confirm. That usually takes a few days, and up to 14.
