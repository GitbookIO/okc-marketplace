## Question

I cancelled a shipment yesterday but the label charge is still on our balance. Did the cancel work?

## Answer

It did. The fee comes back once the carrier confirms the void, which can take up to 14 days. Labels the carrier has already scanned can't be voided, so those aren't refunded.
