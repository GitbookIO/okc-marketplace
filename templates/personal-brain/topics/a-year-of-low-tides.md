A Year of Low Tides is Ilse Brandsma's account of a year on an island off the Frisian coast, after leaving a job she describes only as too many meetings.[^sources-mock-4] It keeps coming back to two things: living by the tide instead of a plan, and learning Frisian by listening. Maya has read chapters 1 to 8.[^sources-mock-12]

## Current understanding

- Chapters 1 and 2: the first thing Brandsma buys is a tide table, and she plans her days around low water. The neighbors don't switch to Dutch for her.[^sources-mock-4]
- Chapters 3 to 5: she learns Frisian at the harbor, by listening for months, and deletes her word app. The ferry runs by the tide, not the clock.[^sources-mock-8]
- Chapters 6 to 8: a winter storm cuts the island off for three days and her plan for the week falls apart. The neighbors only ever plan the next tide.[^sources-mock-12]
- The line Maya wants to keep: you can't hurry a tide, you can only be there when it turns.[^sources-mock-4]
