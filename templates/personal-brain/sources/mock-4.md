## Note

Started A Year of Low Tides (Ilse Brandsma). Picked it up because it's set on an island off the Frisian coast, not far from where Oma grew up.

Ch 1 and 2: she rents a house there for a year after quitting a job she only ever calls "too many meetings" (relatable). First thing she buys: a tide table. Plans her days around low water so she can walk out on the flats.

Keeping this one: you can't hurry a tide, you can only be there when it turns.

No Frisian yet, and the neighbors won't switch to Dutch for her. Brutal. Also kind of great.
