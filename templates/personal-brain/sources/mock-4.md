## Note

Started A Year of Low Tides by Ilse Brandsma. Picked it up because it's set on an island off the Frisian coast, not far from where Oma grew up.

Chapters 1 and 2: Brandsma rents a house on the island for a year after leaving a job she only describes as too many meetings. The first thing she buys is a tide table. She plans her days around low water, when you can walk out on the flats.

Line I want to keep: you can't hurry a tide, you can only be there when it turns.

She doesn't speak Frisian yet, and the neighbors don't switch to Dutch for her.
